Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Balloons Lane collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or submit an inquiry.

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, event date, delivery zip code, occasion details, message and any design file you attach so we can respond to your inquiry and prepare your order.

Log Data

Like many websites, log data may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, date and time of visit and other diagnostic information used to operate and improve the website.

Cookies

Cookies may be used to remember preferences and understand website usage. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies, although some website features may not work as intended.

Third-party service providers may help operate the website, process form submissions, provide maps, videos or analytics, and perform related services. These providers may access personal information only to perform tasks on our behalf.

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable means to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
